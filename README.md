## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Dual Player Mixer** — Two independent players with a mixer for versatile mixing options.
- **Built-in Effects** — Includes reverb, delay, chorus/flanger, and compressor/expander for professional sound.
- **Flexible Mixing Modes** — Choose between manual and automatic mixing modes based on your preference.
- **Multiple Audio Outputs** — Independent outputs for speakers and headphones to streamline your setup.
- **BPM Counter** — Automatic or manual BPM counter to keep your beats aligned.

### Advanced Features:
- **Microphone Input** — Integrate live vocals into your mix effortlessly.
- **CUE Points** — Establish multiple CUE points for precise track manipulation.
- **Loop Controls** — Play loops with dedicated controls for enhanced creativity.
- **Custom Audio Transitions** — Create and edit your audio transitions for unique mixes.
- **Support for Multiple Formats** — Compatible with MP3, AAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG, and FLAC formats.
